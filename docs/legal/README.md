# Legal and commercial documents (drafts)

These are **drafts for review by a lawyer**, written so the facts in them match how the product actually works. They are not legal advice and have not been reviewed by counsel. Every `[bracketed]` item is a decision or a fact to fill in. Do not send them to a customer before they have been reviewed.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [LICENSE_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md](LICENSE_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md) | The software license agreement a customer signs |
| [SUPPORT_POLICY_DRAFT.md](SUPPORT_POLICY_DRAFT.md) | What support includes, how to reach it, how fast |
| [DATA_HANDLING_STATEMENT.md](DATA_HANDLING_STATEMENT.md) | What data the product and the vendor touch, for the customer's own DPDPA and procurement paperwork |

Facts the drafts rely on, so counsel can check them: Veritas is installed and run by the customer on the customer's own infrastructure; the vendor has no access to the customer's installation or data unless the customer sends something; a license is a signed file tied to one machine and valid until its expiry date; the product makes no connection to the vendor.
